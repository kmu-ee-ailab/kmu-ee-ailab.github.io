Our lab has been selected as a Basic Research Laboratory (BRL) by the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF).

**Project:** Next Generation Hyper-perceptive Hyper-adaptive Action Foundation Model Lab
**Funding period:** July 2026 – June 2029

The Basic Research Laboratory program supports sustained, collaborative basic research, and this grant will fund our work toward next-generation action foundation models that perceive richly across modalities and adapt flexibly to new tasks and environments.
