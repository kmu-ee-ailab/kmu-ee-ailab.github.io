Our paper on interpretable retrieval-augmented diagnosis for breast ultrasound, a collaboration with Seoul National University Hospital, has been published in npj Digital Medicine. Lab member Subin An is co-first author.

**Title:** Interpretable Multimodal Retrieval Augmented Diagnosis for Breast Ultrasound with Multinational Clinical Validation and Reader Study
**Authors:** Han Jang, **Subin An**, Sunghyun Jung, Hye Ji, Miran Kim, **Soochahn Lee**, Kyu Sung Choi, Su Min Ha
**Paper:** [npj Digital Medicine article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03272-3)
**Project page:** [B-RAD project page](https://ailab.kookmin.ac.kr/B-RAD-project-page/)
**Code:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/snuh-rad-aicon/B-RAD)

B-RAD mirrors the radiologist workflow of retrieving reference cases, localizing the lesion, and assigning a BI-RADS category. It was validated on 8,311 images from eleven cohorts in seven countries, and in a four-reader study it improved reader accuracy and inter-reader agreement.

Congratulations to all authors!
