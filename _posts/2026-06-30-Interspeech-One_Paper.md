Our collaborative research with the University of Wisconsin-Madison on active speaker detection has been accepted to Interspeech 2026. This is a substantially revised version of our earlier work, *UniTalk*, retitled to better reflect its focus on in-the-wild generalization and robustness.

**Title:** Revisiting Active Speaker Detection: An In-the-Wild Benchmark for Generalization and Robustness
**Authors:** Le Thien Phuc Nguyen, Zhuoran Yu, Khoa Quang Nhat Cao, Yuwei Guo, Tu Ho Manh Pham, Tuan Tai Nguyen, Toan Ngo Duc Vo, Lucas Poon, **Soochahn Lee**, Yong Jae Lee

We look forward to presenting our work at Interspeech 2026!
